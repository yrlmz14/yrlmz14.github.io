Welcome to my technical blog! I wanted a personal publishing space that was completely free of bloated frameworks, heavy runtimes, or excessive dependencies.

## The Architecture

When designing this blog, the requirements were simple:

- **Zero Cost:** No hosting fees, no domain subscription bills, no credit cards required.
- **Minimal Footprint:** Pure HTML5 and CSS3 that loads instantly on any connection.
- **Custom Developer Hosting:** Hosted directly on GitHub Pages with global CDN caching.
- **Continuous Deployment:** Direct version control with Git.
